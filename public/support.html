Common questions

The timer isn't starting when I record.
Make sure you're using cloud recording, not local recording — the app can only detect cloud-recording status. Local recordings aren't visible to Zoom Apps.

The total looks different for different participants.
The total is synced across everyone with the app open, but it can take a few seconds to catch up after a status change. If it's still different after that, try closing and reopening the app panel.

I want to clear the total and start over.
Use the Reset total (everyone) button inside the app — this resets the count for all participants in the meeting. A brand new meeting also always starts at zero automatically.

A window opened that says it isn't attached to a meeting.
This can happen if the app is opened from outside an active call. Close that window and launch the app from the Apps button in the in-meeting toolbar instead, while a meeting is live.
