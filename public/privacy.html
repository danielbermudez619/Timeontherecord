Privacy Policy

Time on the record — Last updated August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Time on the record ("the App") collects when you use it inside Zoom, and how that information is handled.

What the App does

Time on the record is a Zoom App that tracks how long cloud recording has been active during a meeting, and shows that running total to everyone in the meeting who has the App open. The total is synced across participants so it reflects a single, shared record of recording time for the meeting.

Information we collect

  • Meeting identifier. The App uses the meeting's unique ID (meeting UUID) to associate a recording-time total with that specific meeting. No meeting names, topics, or content are collected.
  • Recording status and timestamps. The App reads whether cloud recording is currently active, paused, or stopped, and records the timestamps of those transitions in order to calculate elapsed recording time.
  • Basic Zoom account information. The App requests the user:read:user OAuth scope to identify the authorizing user's basic Zoom profile information (such as name and email) as part of standard Zoom App authorization. This information is not used by any feature of the App itself.
  • Markers you type. If you use the optional "Log a marker" feature, the text you type is stored locally in your own browser session for that meeting and is not sent to our server or shared with other participants.

The App does not access meeting audio, video, chat content, or recordings themselves. It only reads recording status, not recording content.

How information is stored

Recording-time totals are stored on our server, associated with the meeting's UUID, only for the purpose of keeping the total in sync across participants during and after the meeting. This data is not linked to any individual's identity beyond the meeting itself.

How information is used

Information collected is used solely to provide the App's core function: calculating and displaying total recording time for a meeting, shared consistently across participants. We do not use this information for advertising, analytics profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the App's stated function.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share any information collected by the App with third parties. Information is only accessible to the participants of the same meeting, via the shared recording-time total itself.

Data retention

Recording-time totals are retained only as long as needed to serve the meeting they belong to. This data may be cleared when the meeting ends, when a user resets the total from within the App, or periodically as part of routine server maintenance.

Your choices

You can stop using the App at any time by removing it from your Zoom account via the Zoom App Marketplace. Because the App's core function requires reading recording status to work at all, there is no reduced-data mode — declining the required permissions means the App cannot run.
