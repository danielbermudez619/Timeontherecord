Time on the record — Setup and usage guide

Time on the record tracks how long cloud recording has been active during a Zoom meeting, and shows a running total shared live across everyone in the meeting who has the app open.

Adding the app

  1. Install Time on the record from the Zoom App Marketplace to your account.
  2. You'll be asked to authorize the app — this happens inside the Zoom client and only needs to be done once.

Using the app

  1. Start or join a Zoom meeting.
  2. Click Apps in the in-meeting toolbar.
  3. Select Time on the record from the list to open it in the side panel.
  4. Start cloud recording as you normally would — the timer starts automatically and needs no manual input.

The clock pauses automatically if recording is paused, and stops when recording ends. Every participant who has the app open sees the same shared total.

Logging markers

Type a short note into the Log a marker field and press Log (or Enter) to timestamp something worth remembering — a speaker change, an agenda item, and so on. Markers are only visible on the device that logged them.

Resetting the total

Click Reset total (everyone) to clear the running total for all participants in the current meeting. A new meeting always starts its own total at zero automatically — no reset needed between meetings.

Removing the app

Go to the Zoom App Marketplace, find Time on the record under your installed apps, and remove it from there. This revokes the app's access to your account.

Requirements and limitations

  • Works with cloud recording only — local recordings aren't visible to Zoom Apps and can't be tracked.
  • The app must be opened from the in-meeting Apps panel during a live meeting to track that meeting's recording.
